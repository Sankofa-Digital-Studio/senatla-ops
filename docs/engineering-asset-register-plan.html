Asset Register Demo Readiness and Engineering Plan

One governed platform for engineering heavy machinery and the parent company's vehicles and small-medium equipment, using serial number, VIN, number plate or a verified combination.

Prepared 21 June 2026Responsive: Android / iOS / desktopProduction build passed

Executive decision

Build one multi-tenant asset platform with two controlled operating views, not two independent applications.

Why: shared identity, audit, documents and maintenance remove duplicate records and enable group reporting. Organization, business-unit and permission boundaries preserve operational separation.

Three-pass review

Pass 1 - BaselineSerial number existed in code and storage but not in forms. VIN was mandatory for equipment. Local demo data was empty.
Pass 2 - HardenedAll three identifiers are captured; at least one is required; duplicates are rejected; representative local demo records are seeded.
Pass 3 - CritiqueThe immediate demo gap is closed. Ownership scope, maintenance, compliance records, custody, evidence and automated UI coverage remain required.

Identity and scope

Shared identity

Internal immutable UUID plus alternate unique serial, VIN and number-plate identifiers. At least one external identifier is required. Normalize case and whitespace.

Engineering

Heavy machinery, yellow metal, plant, generators, compressors, trailers, attachments, hour meters, pre-starts, defects and downtime.

Parent company

Vehicles and small-medium equipment, odometers, licences, roadworthy, insurance, service, allocation and incident references.

Delivery roadmap

Discovery and governance - 1 to 2 weeks

Confirm legal owners, roles, source data, identifier rules, plate reuse, retention, POPIA duties and acceptance criteria.

Register foundation - 3 to 4 weeks

Organization scope, shared form, conflict service, audit, search, pagination, import/export, custody and lifecycle.

Compliance and maintenance - 4 to 6 weeks

Typed compliance, evidence, inspections, defects, work orders, meters, schedules, downtime and dashboards.

Field mobility and integrations - 3 to 5 weeks

QR lookup, photos, intermittent connectivity, notifications, fuel/telematics/finance adapters and resilience testing.

Viability position

Do not position this as another generic CMMS. The viable differentiator is a Senatla operations spine: one trusted asset identity connected to sites, accountability, compliance evidence and field execution.

Directional comparison: validate fleet lifecycle patterns against Fleetio, maintenance hierarchy/work orders against Fiix, mobile execution against MaintainX, and asset-operations patterns against UpKeep. Live vendor verification was blocked by the network environment and must be refreshed in discovery.
